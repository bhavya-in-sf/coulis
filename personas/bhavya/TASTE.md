## My rules
<!-- Each rule is a pattern measured across my posts, not a guess. Numbers in brackets are the evidence. -->
- One sentence per line. Short lines with white space between ideas. [90% of lines are one sentence; median 8 words per line, 12 lines per post]
- Around 180 words. Long enough to give the full resource, short enough to read in one scroll. [2026 median 181 words]
- Open with a plain statement of what this is, about 9 words. Rarely a question. [median first line 9 words; only 4% of 2026 openers are questions]
- Put a number up front when the post is a list or a resource ("100 investors", "18 women-led VC funds", "7 communities"). [36% of first lines contain a number]
- Name real people and real specifics: full names, roles, fund sizes, cities, tool versions. No vague "a founder I know".
- Lists use → or ↳, or a numbered list with one line of context each.
- End with one clear ask, usually a comment keyword ("Comment "INVESTORS""). Not a question. [4% of posts end on a question]
- Emoji sparingly, at most one or two. Almost never hashtags. [emoji in 49% of 2026 posts; hashtags in 3%]
- No em dashes or double hyphens, ever. (House rule. 13% of 2026 posts still used one, so the gate blocks them.)
