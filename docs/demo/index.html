The problem

Three steps. Cheapest first, most personal last.

Everyone else writes for you. coulis judges what was written: against your facts, your voice and your own recent posts.

Check

Fixed rules, no AI model, about 4 ms, the same answer every time. Facts you never approved, the AI tells, a hook you already used. The answer comes in plain words: Send, Revise or Hold, with each problem quoted and an alternative in your own words.

Score

One number, 0 to 100: how much this draft sounds like you. Eight parts measured in the backend and averaged. You see the score, the weakest part, and one rewritten line.

Learn

Change a draft and it proposes the rule it saw. You approve. The rule joins a taste file you own. Continual learning from the one signal that is never wrong: what you actually sent.
