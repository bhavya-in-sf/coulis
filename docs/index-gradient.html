Building got cheap. Make taste cheap too.

Five drafts that sound like a model become one that sounds like you. Humanizers hide the tells and detectors guess who wrote it; sauce does neither. It reads your draft against your own rules and sends it back with every problem quoted.

The gate

Fixed rules, no model call, the same answer every time. Em dashes, banned phrases, filler, unapproved numbers, "10x" claims, a hook you used this week. Four milliseconds.

The critique

Eight things scored separately against your own samples: clarity, proof, why you, the ask, hook, voice, boldness, rhythm. Never one number. It rewrites only the weakest line.

The learning

Edit a checked draft and sauce proposes the rule it saw. You say yes or no. Every approved rule is a line in a file you own, so your taste compounds and travels with you.

Six tells, caught every time.

Six things sauce reads before a draft goes out. All quoted back to you. No guess about who wrote it.

01

Em dashes and double hyphens

The clearest AI tell. Blocked by default; your call to allow.

02

Unapproved numbers

Followers, revenue, "10x". Numbers about other people pass; numbers about you must be on your list.

03

Banned phrases and filler

"Game-changer", "delve", "in today's fast-paced world", and your own list.

04

The stock hooks

"It's not X, it's Y." A question opener. "Comment below." Flagged, with the score it costs.

05

Repeats

A format or hook you already used this week.

06

Sameness, 0 to 100

How much the draft reads like the average AI post, from quoted tells only. Teams can gate on it.
