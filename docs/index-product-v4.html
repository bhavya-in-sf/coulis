Blocks the em dash and the filler.

Em and en dashes, double hyphens, "game-changer", "delve", "in today's fast-paced world". Your banned list, plus the defaults.

Sends back any number you have not approved.

Followers, users, revenue, percentages, "10x". Numbers about other people pass. Six revenue figures caught on one team's drafts.

Learns a rule from every edit.

Edit a checked draft and sauce proposes the rule it saw. You say yes or no. Every approved rule is a line in a file you own.

Sends Claude back if it skipped the check.

A hook at the end of every reply. If a draft went out unchecked, Claude is sent back once to run it.

Scores eight things. Never one number.

Clarity, proof, why you, the ask, hook, voice, boldness, rhythm. It rewrites only the weakest line, so the result is still yours.

For five years my job was reading founders' drafts and sending them back.

Two agencies, 38 voices, a few thousand posts. The work was never the writing. It was the moment before posting: is that number real, does this sound like you, would you say this to a colleague. I did that check by hand, every day, for other people.

Then AI made the drafts free and the check disappeared. Every post I read in 2026 had the same dashes, the same hooks, numbers nobody had approved. The tools that promised "your voice" produced everyone's voice. So I wrote the check down as rules and a file, and put it between the draft and the send button.

It checked 673 of my own posts first. A third of them would not have passed. That is the point.

Bhavya GoelSan Francisco, October 2026
