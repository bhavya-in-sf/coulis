    AI drafts arrive fast and all sound alike: 53.7% of long-form LinkedIn posts from influential profiles in 2025 read as likely AI (Originality.ai, Jan 2026). sauce puts one check between the draft and the send button. Everything passes through your taste, then comes out in your voice.

    Draft

    Five writers, one agent, or just you at 11pm. The drafts come in every voice but yours.

    Taste

    One check. Your approved facts, your banned phrases, your rules. Eight axes, scored one by one, never averaged.

    Send

    Out comes writing in your voice. Edit it anyway, and sauce learns the rule you just taught it. You approve every rule.

    What a check looks like

    The draft number

    Every draft gets one. Checks are logged, so you can see what sauce caught this month.

    The stamp

    BLOCK must be fixed. FIX should be. PASS ships. Nothing is scored out of 100.

    The number check

    Any figure not on your approved list is sent back. Numbers about other people pass.

    Draft #214X post · 14:02
    FIX
    • FIX"$50k MRR"
      Not on your approved list. Add it, or cut it.
    • BLOCK"game-changer"
      Banned. Your rule, 12 Oct.
    • TASTEHook 5 · Voice 8 · Proof 4 · Ask 9
      Weakest: Proof. Rewrite below keeps your voice.
    Learned from your last edit: "You cut the opening question. Open with the number." Approve?
    The banned list

    Words you never want to see again. You add them in plain English: "ban game-changer."

    Eight axes

    Clarity, proof, why you, the ask are firm. Hook, voice, boldness, rhythm follow your samples. Only the weakest line gets rewritten.

    It learns from your edits

    Edit before posting, and sauce proposes the rule it learned. You say yes or no.
