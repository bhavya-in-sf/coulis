The gate

Run every draft through your rules.

Fixed rules, no model call, the same answer every time. It reads the draft and sends it back with each problem quoted.

  • Em dashes, double hyphens, filler and your banned phrases
  • Any number, money figure or "10x" claim you have not approved
  • A format you already used this week

The critique

Let the check handle the busywork.

Eight things scored separately, against your own samples. Never one number. It rewrites only the weakest line.

  • Clarity, proof, why you, the ask
  • Hook, voice, boldness, rhythm
  • Bias and private data flagged on the side
