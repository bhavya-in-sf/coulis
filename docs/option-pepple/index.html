The problem

AI sounds like everyone else.

It does not reliably express your creativity, individuality or taste. You re-explain yourself, and it still comes out average. These are the patterns Pepple quotes back to you.

How it works

Three steps. You approve every one.

The gate: fixed rules, no AI model, the same answer every time, about 4 ms. The critique: 8 things scored separately (clarity, proof, why you, the ask, hook, voice, boldness, rhythm), never one overall number, and only the weakest line is rewritten. The learning: edit a draft, it proposes a rule, you approve it.

Your taste, in a file you own

Your facts. Your voice. Your file.

Approved facts and voice rules live in a plain file on your machine. It grows only when you say yes.

Written for the moment

A cold email is not a launch post.

Founders and teams write launch posts, LinkedIn and X posts, cold emails, bios and investor updates. Each is checked with its own rules.

Research

What we used. What it showed.
