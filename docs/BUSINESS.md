# Business shape

No revenue today. This page describes the planned shape, labelled as planned.

**B2B2C.** coulis is sold to the apps, agents and assistants people already use, and used directly inside Claude and Cursor today. Either way, the person behind the account is who the reader hears.

**Free, and staying free**
- The open-source plug-in. That is the distribution.

**Paid, in order of planned arrival**
1. Enterprise pilots: a company's brand strategy and taste file built from its own data, memory and approved facts, into a custom model that runs coulis, integrated into the systems it already uses (ERP, CRM, content tools). Custom work on their data is what is chargeable. No pilot signed yet.
2. Team tier for agencies and startups: shared profiles, approved claims, the team score gate.
3. The API, priced per check, for AI apps and agents writing under someone's name.

**Market context, ranges not forecasts.** AI writing assistant software is sized at $1.3B to $6B in 2026 depending on the analyst; a bottom-up estimate is about $844M a year across creators, founders and teams. Taste Labs raised an $18.5M seed for taste at the model layer; design-taste add-ons for Claude passed 969K installs in 2026. Writing has no funded leader inside the tools.
