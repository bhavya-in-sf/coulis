# Coulis business model: B2B2C, VC-first

Founder-selected strategy: raise venture capital to build a taste LLM and its infrastructure, while charging from the first commercial engagement. This is a plan, not a claim of booked revenue, signed customers or funding already raised.

## Who buys, who uses, who benefits

Coulis → AI writing/communication-agent company → that company's end users.

The business buys integration, evaluation and eventually model inference. End users receive writing and judgments conditioned on their own context and preferences inside products they already use. Agencies can be distribution/research partners; Coulis does not become a bespoke copywriting agency. End users need not buy a separate Coulis subscription for this model to work.

## Revenue from day one of commercial delivery

**Initial paid design-partner offer:** a bounded local prototype integration and evaluation engagement. Deliver a working integration using currently available Python/MCP capabilities, separate authorized user profiles, a defined baseline comparison, and an integration/evaluation report. State acceptance criteria, duration, support and price in a signed scope before work. This is hands-on research/integration work, not access to an already trained taste LLM or hosted enterprise platform.

Use an upfront fee for the scoped engagement and an optional separately agreed recurring integration/support arrangement. No price or customer commitment has been validated yet. Do not promise an unavailable hosted product to collect revenue.

**Later recurring offer:** hosted taste-LLM generation, personalized ranking/critique and taste-state infrastructure, priced against measured inference cost and value. Consider a platform minimum plus metered usage. Distinguish one-time pilot fees from recurring software/model revenue when reporting traction.

## VC-first funding

Fundraise now around the research thesis, team, experimental plan and B2B2C distribution. Venture capital funds technical research ownership/hiring, licensed compute, authorized data work, model training/evaluation and production infrastructure. Do not wait for customer revenue to finance the entire research program.

Paid pilots run alongside fundraising. Their purpose is commercial validation and useful distribution feedback, not proof of model superiority. Investor materials must separately report capital sought, capital committed, cash received, pilot contracts, actual revenue, recurring revenue and active end users. Those figures are currently unconfirmed.

## First launch CTA

Primary commercial CTA: **Become a paid design partner** → form for a scoped pilot discussion. No checkout or service provisioning is live. Research CTA: **Explore the taste LLM**. Prototype CTA: **Try the local preview**. Hosted model/API access remains waitlist only.

## Data rights in B2B2C

A business contract does not automatically authorize use of all its end users' writing for shared training. The partner must have authority for the specified service processing; optional shared research/training needs appropriate separate authorization and transparent end-user handling. Keep client/user boundaries, provenance, permitted purposes and deletion responsibilities explicit. No automatic pooling of partner data into a common taste model.

## Validation

Measure partner integration and paid conversion separately from end-user activation, retention, preference wins, editing effort and factual regressions. Model quality and unit economics need their own evidence. A paid pilot can validate willingness to pay for today's work without validating tomorrow's trained model.
