Your editor that never writes.

Train it on your facts and your voice once. Then it reads everything, in every tool, before it goes out. A checker cannot make you generic; the words stay yours.

saucev0.6.2 · MIT
  • Reads your approved facts. Any number not on the list is sent back.
  • Knows your banned words, your filler, and a format you used this week.
  • Scores eight things separately, never one number. Rewrites only the weakest line.
  • Learns from what you edit. You approve every rule.
  • Lives in Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor and OpenClaw. Sends Claude back if it skipped the check.
  • Joins your Slack. It reads the drafts channel and checks every message before anyone hits post. Read-only today, so nothing is sent for you.

Six jobs, one profile.

Put sauce to work. Each job is tied to a result from the tests, not a promise.

The gate

Em dashes, filler, banned phrases, unapproved numbers and money claims, "10x" claims, a format you reused this week. Deterministic, 4 ms, no model.

17 team drafts replayed: 9 stopped, 2 sent back, 6 passed.

Critique

Clarity, proof, why you, the ask, hook, voice, boldness, rhythm. Scored apart against your own samples. Bias and private data flagged on the side.

41 of 44 held-out posts scored higher with their author's profile.

Learn

Compares your draft with what you posted and proposes the rule. You say yes or no.

Rules carry the date they were learned.

Joins your Slack

Point it at the channel where your team drafts. Every message gets the same check as the founder's own posts, with the last seven days as the sameness memory. Read-only: it reports, it never posts.

examples/slack_shadow.py · 47 team drafts checked this way, 46 pass after one approval.

Deck checker

Reads a PDF, PPTX or Markdown deck slide by slide and flags the same noun with different numbers across slides.

scripts/deck_check.py

Team gate and the nudge

They ship style in one app. Your taste should travel.

Every lab now has a style box and a memory. All of it is typed by hand, locked to one app and one user, and never checked against the finished draft. sauce is the check, and the profile goes with you into Claude, Cursor, OpenClaw and any agent that calls it.

Your agents speak as you. sauce is the check they run first.

  • Remix, Puff, Wajo, OpenClaw: personal agents now post, email and message under their owner's name. None of them checks the draft against the person.
  • One call, deterministic, local, about 4 ms. No model, no cost per check.
  • Never invents a number to satisfy a check. Asks the owner instead.
