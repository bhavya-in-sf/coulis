Our name / Our point of view

coulis / koo-LEE / noun

A smooth sauce made from fruit or vegetables. Concentrated flavor. A finishing touch that changes the whole.

That is the idea behind Coulis: AI that can learn the choices that make something feel right to a particular person, in a particular moment.

04 / Clear boundaries

Ambition, with evidence.

What exists today?

An open-source local prototype with configured checks, explicit preference approval and context selection. Provider-based critique uses your selected model. No trained Coulis taste LLM is released.

Capabilities & ownership ↗
What learns, and with whose permission?

Approved preference memory can update independently of model weights. Training a shared model requires separate permission. Customer writing is not automatically pooled into a training corpus.

Data permissions ↗
What has been validated?

The public synthetic fixture tests deterministic behavior. We have not yet run independent user studies, measured editing-time savings, or validated the eight-axis model critique.
