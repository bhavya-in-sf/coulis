Why coulis

Concentrated flavor.
Distinct character.

A coulis is a smooth sauce made from fruit or vegetables. A base or a finishing touch, it brings another dimension to a dish.

That is our starting metaphor: AI should bring out your character. Our research asks how a model can learn your choices without blending them into everyone else’s.

Coulis Research

A language model.
A sense of your taste.

Our goal is a taste LLM that generates, critiques and ranks language according to a person and a context. We plan to compare post-training, personal conditioning and continual adaptation against strong writing-model baselines. The taste LLM is not trained or released; no user outcome study has been completed. Read the research agenda.

GenerateResearch goal: produce language that reflects individual judgment, beyond stylistic imitation.
ChooseResearch goal: critique and rank alternatives against the person’s contextual preferences.
AdaptResearch goal: learn from authorized feedback without forgetting or mixing people’s preferences.
EvaluateBlinded human comparisons, held-out topics, factual checks and published limitations. Studies are not yet run.

How we keep it honest

Approval first. Private by default.

Approval first

It never sends or rewrites without your yes. Every change comes with an explanation. Nothing is added to your taste file without a yes.

Privacy and security

Local checks stay on your machine; model critique shares selected context with your provider. The basic check never calls a model. Hosted enterprise security is not live. Data permissions policy.

Benchmarking and validation

Automated tests check specified behavior. They do not establish better writing. Our human evaluation protocol compares Coulis with an equally informed native model.
