# Agency pilot — prepared, not launched

Taste infrastructure is the product, not a later pivot. Target first paying customers: small AI writing/agent product teams that need persistent user judgment across models. Recruit agencies managing 3–10 founder voices as design partners and evaluation environments. The buyer pays for the reusable preference/check/critique layer; users supply authorized feedback. Individuals can use the local open-source tool. Hosted paid access remains waitlist only.

Sequence: integrate the local layer into 1–2 writing/agent products → measure benefit with their consenting users and agency design partners → offer hosted infrastructure once value and reliability are established. Pilot access does not imply a current hosted API or enterprise security offering.

## Four-week protocol

Week 0: recruit 3–5 agencies/10 writers with permission; record current tool, client count, baseline editing time, recurring corrections and willingness to pay. No outreach has been sent. Obtain service-use authority and optional evaluation permission separately.
Week 1: onboard separate client folders with three authorized samples each; no export required. Record setup time. Run the three-condition blind baseline in BENCHMARK.md.
Weeks 2–3: review scoped learning proposals; track recurrence on unseen topics, inappropriate carryover, facts missed, time spent correcting Coulis, and voluntary weekly use. Never treat a sent draft as approval for a permanent preference.
Week 4: repeat blinded evaluation on reserved topics; include dropouts and negative outcomes. Ask whether each agency will pay to continue. Report results before deciding on hosted teams/API work.

Planning hypothesis only: 10–25% less active editing time after setup in repetitive workflows; initial use may save nothing or take longer. This is not a forecast supported by tests. Measure setup-adjusted savings and reviewer time, not just generation latency. Do not put this range in customer claims.

## Suggested decision gates, to pre-register

Continue only if writers prefer the Coulis condition over the equally informed native baseline, factual-error outcomes do not worsen, and a majority of participating agencies voluntarily return and at least one integrating product team agrees to a paid infrastructure pilot when hosted access is ready. These are internal pilot decisions, not statistical proof or YC requirements. Report sample uncertainty; do not scale a percentage from a tiny cohort.

Use evals/pilot-template.csv for records. Keep actual writing in permissioned local storage; never commit client outputs. Do not activate automatic collection or send invites without a chosen cohort and authorization.
