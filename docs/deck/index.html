Insight

Your edits reveal your taste. What you keep, change and reject.

What we measureEvery edit you make is a labelled example: the draft, the version you sent, the context it was for. Not what you say you like. What you actually changed.
What we do with itThe edit becomes a proposed rule in plain words. You approve it or not. Approved rules join a taste file you own and are applied by audience and format: an investor update is not a launch post.
The hypothesisEditorial decisions captured this way improve how much a draft on a topic you have never written about sounds like you, beyond a strong model given the same samples. Measured so far: 41 of 44 held-out posts. The unseen-topic test is next.
Status: edits and approvals captured today; unseen-topic transfer and a trained taste model are research, not product. docs/RESEARCH-AGENDA.md

How it works

Three steps. Cheapest first, most personal last.

Draft · LinkedIn · 14:02checked in 4 ms
HOLDFour lines to settle: em dash, "game-changer", "300+ founders", "10x". Each quoted, with a fix in your words.
CheckFixed rules, no AI model, about 4 ms, the same answer every time. Facts you never approved, the AI tells, a hook you already used. The answer comes in plain words, Send, Revise or Hold, with each problem quoted and an alternative in your own words.
ScoreOne number, 0 to 100: how much this draft sounds like you. Eight parts measured in the backend and averaged. You see the score, the weakest part, and one rewritten line.
LearnChange a draft and it proposes the rule it saw. You approve. The rule joins a taste file you own. Continual learning from the one signal that is never wrong: what you actually sent.
Continual learningPrivacySecurityBenchmarkingValidationApproval first
Onboarding: one command builds your taste file from your LinkedIn data export (Settings, Data privacy, Get a copy of your data, Posts; about 10 minutes). No export: paste three posts and answer five questions. Privacy and security: your taste file stays on your machine; the basic check never calls a model; the API hashes keys and encrypts profiles and never logs drafts. Benchmarking and validation: known drafts with known answers before every release, and posts the system has never seen.
