# The axes

Every draft is scored on eight axes, in two groups. There is never one overall score. Each axis maps to a source in [EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md).

## Why two groups
Professional evaluators agree on checkable qualities and split on taste-driven ones (Human Creativity Benchmark, EVIDENCE E2). So checkable axes are held firm, and taste axes follow the user's TASTE.md.

## Why no single score
Taste scorers that collapse separate judgments into one number lose the most useful information (EVIDENCE E2, E3). The critic reports each axis on its own.

## Group 1: Checkable (firm for everyone)

| Axis | Question | 1 looks like | 10 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clarity | Can a stranger say what this is about after one read? | Several ideas, jargon | One idea, plain words |
| Proof | Is every claim backed by something specific (a number, a name, a result)? | "We help teams move faster" | "Cut onboarding from 9 days to 2" |
| Why you | Does it say something only this person or company could say? | Any competitor could post it | Only this founder could have written it |
| Ask | Does the reader know the one next action? | No ask, or three asks | One concrete action |

## Group 2: Taste (steerable, follows TASTE.md)

| Axis | Question | Default direction (seed) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Does line one earn line two? | One line, result up front |
| Voice | Does it sound like this person on their best day? | Matches TASTE.md samples |
| Boldness | Does it take a position? | A direct opinion where it earns its place |
| Rhythm | Do line lengths and sentences vary? | Mixed short and long lines |

Defaults in Group 2 come from practitioner experience (EVIDENCE P1). A user's TASTE.md always overrides them.
