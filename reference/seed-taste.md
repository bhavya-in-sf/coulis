# Seed taste (expert defaults)

Used only until the user's TASTE.md says otherwise. Source type: Practitioner (EVIDENCE P1). These rules come from 5 years of editing founder and personal-brand posts. They are experience, not research.

## Slop to flag
- Corporate language ("leverage", "synergy", "unlock", "empower", "game-changer")
- Generic encouragement ("You've got this!", "The possibilities are endless")
- Rhetorical contrast templates ("It's not X. It's Y.")
- Triple-denial hooks ("No fluff. No hype. No BS.")
- Rule-of-three phrasing used as filler
- Vague attributions ("Studies show", "Experts say") with no source
- Hedging ("might", "perhaps", "it could be argued") where a position is warranted
- AI filler ("In today's fast-paced world", "Let's dive in", "delve")
- Writing about the post instead of the topic ("In this post, I'll share")

## Moves that tend to work
- Hook: one line, the result or number first.
- Proof: two sharply contrasting data points beat a list of five.
- Use numerals for numbers, even small ones.
- One idea per post.
- End on one concrete action.

## Never
- Invent experience, numbers or stories the user did not provide. If proof is missing, ask for it or mark the gap.
